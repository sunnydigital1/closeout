Make real sandbox activity to reconcile.

Every purchase here is a real PayPal sandbox payment, and each normal sale is written to your books the way a shop's order system would. A few buttons then create the mistakes Closeout should catch.

In the PayPal window, log in with your sandbox buyer account. You'll find it in the PayPal developer dashboard under Testing Tools, Sandbox Accounts. No real money moves.

  1. Create the wholesale invoices

    Four invoices through PayPal's Invoicing API: one paid by bank transfer, one partly paid, one due tomorrow (it turns overdue after that), and one not due yet.

  2. Make a few normal sales

    Five or six is plenty. Each one is recorded in your books.

  3. Create the mistakes

    Each of these leaves PayPal and your books disagreeing, the way it happens in real shops.

    The customer pays twice

    Charges an order again, as if checkout went through twice. Your books keep one sale.

    A sale that skips your books

    A $75.00 custom-order deposit paid by PayPal link, with no order behind it.

    A refund done only in PayPal

    Refunds an order through the PayPal API without updating your books.

  4. Run the close

    PayPal can take up to three hours to show new payments in its transaction search. After that, open Closeout, switch to PayPal sandbox, and click Run the close.
